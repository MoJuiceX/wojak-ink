# Background Art Prompts — Combat Type Themed Scenes

## Art Style Analysis (from existing backgrounds)

After studying all 16 existing backgrounds, here is the EXACT style:

- **Format:** 1000x1000px PNG, opaque (no transparency)
- **Drawing style:** Extremely simple, almost MS Paint-level cartoon. Think a step above stick figures. NOT polished illustration — deliberately crude and charming.
- **Outlines:** Thick dark outlines (3-4px) on everything. Every shape is clearly outlined.
- **Coloring:** Flat solid color fills. NO gradients, NO shading, NO highlights, NO texture. Each area is one single flat color.
- **Shapes:** Very basic geometric. Trees = green circles on brown rectangles. Clouds = white blobs with black outline. Mountains = triangles. Ground = flat color band across the bottom.
- **Complexity:** Extremely low. Most backgrounds have only 3-6 distinct objects. Lots of empty space filled with flat color.
- **Perspective:** Simple vanishing point or completely flat. No complex 3D.
- **Detail level:** Almost none. A bookshelf is just colored rectangles inside a rectangle. Server racks are grey boxes with colored dots. Craters are simple ovals.

### Style References from Existing Backgrounds:
- **Orange Grove:** Blue sky, white blob clouds with outline, green circle-trees on brown sticks, flat green grass, flat ground. That's it.
- **Chia Farm:** Blue sky, white clouds, brown dirt with simple one-point perspective rows, tiny green leaf sprouts. Very few elements.
- **Nesting Grounds:** Blue sky, white clouds, grey triangle mountains, row of simple green triangle-trees, flat blue water with a few black squiggle lines, green bushes at edges.
- **Route 66:** Pale sky gradient (light blue to peach), dark grey road going to vanishing point, flat orange-brown desert ground, a few tiny green bush tufts, simple ground texture squiggles.
- **Bepe Barracks:** Blue sky, white clouds, flat green hill, flat sandy ground with squiggle marks, two shooting target signs on sticks. Very minimal.
- **Moon:** Black space with tiny dot stars, flat grey lunar surface, simple oval craters, small Earth icon (blue-green circle).
- **Hell:** Repeating flame shapes in red/orange/yellow with black outlines. Pattern-based, no scene composition.
- **Spell Room:** Stone wall = blue-grey rectangles with dark outlines. Stone arch = simple curved shape. Bookshelves = brown rectangles with colored rectangle books. Purple crystal ball = circle. Candles = simple white rectangles with yellow flame dots.

- **White House:** Simple building outline, oval seal, two American flag shapes. Flat blue background.

## Post-Generation Checklist

1. Resize to exactly **1000x1000px**
2. Save as **PNG**
3. Verify thick black outlines on ALL shapes
4. Verify flat solid color fills — NO gradients, NO shading, NO texture
5. Verify no characters or faces
6. Verify it looks like it belongs next to Orange Grove and Chia Farm — if it looks too detailed or too "professional", regenerate with "even simpler, fewer details, more crude cartoon, like a meme background"
7. If ChatGPT keeps making it too detailed, try adding: "Draw this like a child would draw it but with thick clean black outlines and flat bold colors. No rendering, no lighting effects, no 3D."
