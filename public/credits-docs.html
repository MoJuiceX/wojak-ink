Credits: Formula & Ops

How free-mint credits are calculated and how the system is monitored.

Formula

credits = 50 × (price ÷ floor) × (1 + 0.2 × ln(price ÷ floor))
price = XCH you paid · floor = floor price at purchase · ln = natural log

Only XCH buys earn credits; CAT-only trades are excluded. Stored values use hundredths (divide by 100 for display). One free mint = 100 credits = 10,000 stored units.
