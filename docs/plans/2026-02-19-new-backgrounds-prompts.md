## How to Use These Prompts in ChatGPT (4o)

### The #1 Rule: Upload a Reference Image Every Time
ChatGPT 4o has no memory between sessions. **Every single time** you start a new chat to generate a background, you MUST upload one of the existing backgrounds (Spell Room or Graveyard work best) and say:

> *"Use this image as the exact art style reference. I want the same thick black outlines, same flat solid color fills with zero gradients or shading, same crude cartoon simplicity. Now generate this scene: [paste prompt]"*

This is the single most powerful technique for locking style consistency.

### The Style Lock Prefix
Every prompt below already includes this prefix built in. Do NOT remove it. If ChatGPT drifts toward a more polished look, add this at the end:

> *"Even simpler. Draw it like a child drew it but with clean thick black outlines. No rendering, no lighting, no shadows, no gradients. Flat color only. More crude."*

### Iteration Tips
- **Change one thing at a time.** If the style is right but the layout is wrong, say "same style, but move the X to the left and make it smaller."
- **Lock the style first.** Generate a version you like, then say "keep this exact art style and regenerate with [tweak]."
- **If it keeps getting too detailed:** Add `"MS Paint level simplicity"` or `"even fewer objects, more empty space"` to your prompt.
- **Size:** Always specify `1000x1000 square` — ChatGPT tends to do 16:9 by default.
- **No characters:** All prompts specify no characters or faces. If any appear, say "remove all characters, people, and faces."

## THE COMPOSITION RULE — READ THIS FIRST

**The Wojak character face and upper body will be placed in the CENTER and BOTTOM CENTER of the image at all times.**

This means:
- The **center-bottom 40% of the image** will be covered by the Wojak's face and chest
- The **dead center of the image** will be covered by the Wojak's head

**Every background must be composed like a stage set:**
- ✅ Objects go in the **top-left corner**, **top-right corner**, **far left edge**, **far right edge**, **top center**
- ✅ The **center-bottom area must be empty** — flat ground, flat floor, flat water, flat road — nothing there
- ✅ The **left/right sides frame the character** like curtains or wings of a stage
- ❌ Never place key objects in the center of the image
- ❌ Never place objects in the bottom half center — they will be hidden behind the face

**The best existing examples of this:**
- **Spell Room** — bookshelves left and right, giant empty arch in the center = perfect
- **Colosseum** — wall curves around edges, red flags top-left and top-right, empty sandy floor in center-bottom = perfect
- **Ronin Dojo** — trees far left and far right, building recedes in background, flat path in center-bottom = perfect
- **Graveyard** — tombstones left-center, tree far right, moon top-right, open dark ground center-bottom = perfect
- **Dark Alley** — dumpster bottom-left corner, TV top-right wall, empty wall in center = perfect

**The composition fix phrase** — if ChatGPT puts objects in the wrong place, say:
> *"Move all objects to the left edge, right edge, or top corners. The center and center-bottom must be completely empty — that area will be covered by a character's face. Think of it as a stage backdrop where the actor stands in the middle."*
